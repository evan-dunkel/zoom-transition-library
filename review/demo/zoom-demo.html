Each framed area below (a “stage”) is a small, separate website built with the library, completely unmodified. Click the cards inside a stage to try it. Everything runs from this one file: no internet connection, installs or server needed.

Use Slow motion (top right) to judge the motion frame by frame. A dark status bar at the bottom of each stage shows what the library is doing. When nothing is happening, it should read clean ✓, meaning nothing was left behind on the page.

Scenario 1

Card grid zooming into detail pages

The main use: a grid of cards. Clicking one zooms its picture up into a full detail page. The neighbouring stories peek in at the sides, and you can move between them.

What to look for

  • ShouldThe picture should leave from exactly where the card is and land exactly at the top of the detail page. I measured it to the pixel: it does.
  • ShouldClose with Esc, the ✕ button, a click on the dark area above the card, or by dragging the card down from its top edge with the mouse. It should shrink back into its own card, and the other cards should fly home too.
  • Should← and → move between stories. At either end there is a small bounce.
  • ShouldResize the browser window while a card is open. The card should re-fit to the new size.
  • ProblemTurn on Slow motion and watch the very first moment of the zoom. The thumbnail’s rounded corners turn square in one frame, and the picture inside shifts. The flying picture is the detail page’s wider image, cropped differently from the thumbnail.
  • ProblemThe ✕ button pops in only at the very end, because the flying picture covers it until it lands.
  • ProblemResize the window while a card is still zooming open. The card stays at the old size, partly off-screen, until you resize again.
  • ProblemTry to select text in an open story. You can’t: all text in the open view is unselectable.

Scenario 2

A scrolled page with a sticky header

A long page with a header that stays stuck to the top. The cards are further down, so you have to scroll before opening one.

What to look for

  • ShouldScroll down to “Featured walks” and open a card. It should still leave from exactly the right place. I measured it: it does.
  • ShouldWhile a card is open, the page behind can’t scroll (on purpose). After closing, you should be at the same scroll position as before.
  • ProblemScroll so a card is half hidden under the sticky header, then open it. The hidden half jumps out on top of the header in one frame. On close, the picture lands on top of the header, then snaps underneath it.

Scenario 3

Narrow, phone-width layout (390 px)

A phone-width list. Small square thumbnails zoom up into full-screen pages, which is a big change of shape.

What to look for

  • ShouldThe zoom should start from the small square and end exactly at the top of the page.
  • ShouldWith a mouse, drag the open card down from its top edge to close it, or drag sideways to move between stories. For real touch gestures, press “Open in full window” and turn on your browser’s device mode. In Chrome that’s DevTools → the phone icon, or Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+M.
  • ProblemThe rounded-corner pop and the picture shift at take-off are easier to see here, because the shape changes so much (square to wide).

Scenario 4

Reduced motion turned on (simulated)

This frame pretends your device has Reduce motion turned on, so you can see the result without changing any settings.

What to look for

  • ShouldOpening and closing should be a plain cross-fade. Nothing should move, grow or shrink. I measured it: nothing moves.
  • Should← and → should switch stories instantly, without sliding.
  • ProblemIf you change the real setting on your device while this page is open, the library ignores the change until you reload the page.

How to turn on reduced motion for real

macOS
System Settings → Accessibility → Display → Reduce motion.
Windows 11
Settings → Accessibility → Visual effects → Animation effects: Off.
iPhone / iPad
Settings → Accessibility → Motion → Reduce Motion.
Android
Settings → Accessibility → Remove animations (the wording varies by phone).
Chrome only (no system change)
DevTools (F12) → ⋮ menu → More tools → Rendering → “Emulate CSS media feature prefers-reduced-motion” → reduce.

Then reload this page. The status bar in every stage should read “reduced motion: ON (your OS)”, and every scenario should cross-fade instead of zooming.

Scenario 5

Rapid repeated clicks and interruptions

The buttons in the frame run quick sequences (double-click, eight fast clicks, Esc or Back halfway through, and so on). Try them yourself as well. Browser history is turned on in this scenario, so the browser’s Back button closes the card.

What to look for

  • ShouldAfter every test, the dark status bar at the bottom should read clean ✓ once things settle. If a card is still open, press Esc first. I ran all of these tests automatically, and every one ended clean.
  • ShouldClick a card as it shrinks back home: it should turn around and open again smoothly, without restarting.
  • ShouldPress “Open in full window” to see the address bar. Opening a card adds #rapid-2 (for example) to the address, and the browser’s Back button closes it.
  • ProblemIn the full-window version, reload while a card is open, or copy the address into a new tab. The card does not reopen, and the address keeps a stale #…. Deep links only work if your site has real pages at those addresses.

Scenario 6

Photos that load late (critical bug)

The detail pages use real photos that take about a second to “download” the first time. Odd-numbered cards (1, 3, 5) don’t reserve space for the photo. Even-numbered cards (2, 4, 6) do, using CSS aspect-ratio. That’s how most real sites behave on a first visit.

What to look for

  • ShouldCards 2, 4 and 6 (“Photo space reserved”) zoom normally. The photo fades into place once it arrives.
  • ProblemOpen card 1, 3 or 5. The zoom gets stuck. The photo area stays blank, a stray copy of the image stays on screen, and the status bar never returns to idle. Press Esc: the page stays frozen, can’t scroll, and the card stays missing. Only a reload fixes it. Press Reload stage to recover.

Scenario 7

A source that is partly off-screen (sideways row)

A sideways-scrolling row of cards, where the last visible card is cut off by the edge of the row.

What to look for

  • ShouldOpen the cut-off card. It should zoom from where it actually is, half-hidden.
  • ProblemOpen a card, press → a few times to reach a story whose card is off-screen in the row, then close. The row jumps instantly to show that card before the zoom flies home, instead of scrolling smoothly.

Scenario 8

Keyboard and screen reader

Detail pages with a text field and a link, to test keyboard use. Click inside the frame first, then use only the keyboard.

What to look for

  • ShouldTab to a card and press Enter. Focus (the outline) should move to the ✕ button. Press Esc: the card closes and focus returns to the card you opened.
  • ProblemWith a card open, press Tab three times. Focus leaves the card and goes to the hidden page behind it (the header links). A keyboard user is now lost.
  • ProblemClick in the note field and press ← or →. Instead of moving the text cursor, it switches to another story. Esc inside the field closes the whole card.
  • ProblemScreen readers aren’t told that anything opened, and the open view has no name. On a Mac, turn on VoiceOver with Cmd+F5. On Windows, turn on Narrator with Ctrl+Win+Enter.
