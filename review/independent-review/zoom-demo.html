See the zoom transitions for yourself

Each box below is a small, separate website running the library exactly as it is in the repository (nothing changed). Under each one is a checklist. Items marked Should work are things I measured as correct; please confirm they look right to you. Problem I found items are issues from my report; please confirm you can see them. Your judgement items need a designer's eye.

When you're done, press Copy results at the bottom and paste the text back to me. Your answers are kept if you reload this page in the same browser. The dark bar at the bottom of each frame is a diagnostic I added (it's not part of the library): it shows whether anything was left behind after a transition, the slowest frame, and where keyboard focus is.

1. Card grid zooming into detail pages

A grid of walks. Click a picture: it flies into a detail card while the card grows around it. The neighbouring walks peek in at the sides (← → or a sideways trackpad swipe moves between them). Close with Esc, the ✕, a click on the dimmed area, or by scrolling up past the top of the card.

What to look for

  1. Should work The picture leaves exactly from its thumbnail and lands exactly at the top of the card, with no jump at either end. (Turn on slow motion at the top to see it clearly.)
  2. Should work Closing sends the picture back into its own thumbnail, and the page looks exactly as before.
  3. Your judgement The motion feels good to you: speed, slight bounce, the way the card grows out of the picture.
  4. Problem I found At the moment you click, the other thumbnails on the page blink out (most visible on the second row), and stay hidden until the card has closed.
  5. Problem I found In slow motion, the ✕ button can be seen floating just outside the top-right of the growing card during the first part of the opening.
  6. Your judgement Page turns (← →): try the “Page turn” switch at the top of this page. Which setting feels right on your display? (Say which in the note.)

2. A scrolled page with a sticky header

A long page whose header stays stuck to the top. The cards are further down: scroll to “Featured walks” before opening one.

What to look for

  1. Should work After scrolling, the zoom still starts exactly from the thumbnail you clicked.
  2. Should work After closing, you are at the same scroll position as before.
  3. Problem I found Scroll so a thumbnail is half hidden under the header, then click its title. The hidden half pops out on top of the header in the first frame (and on closing it lands on top, then snaps underneath).

3. Narrow phone-width layout (390 px)

A phone-width list with small square thumbnails that open into full-height cards: a big change of shape. For touch, use “Open in new tab” and your browser's device mode (Chrome: Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+M), or open the file on a phone.

What to look for

  1. Should work The picture grows from the small square into the wide picture at the top of the card, its crop changing smoothly with no jump.
  2. Should work Closing shrinks it back into its own square.
  3. Your judgement (Touch, optional) Dragging down from the top of the card closes it; dragging sideways moves to the next walk.

4. Reduced motion (simulated in this frame)

This frame pretends your device has “Reduce motion” turned on. To test the real setting, turn it on (instructions at the bottom of this page) and use the other frames: they follow your device setting.

What to look for

  1. Should work Opening is instant: the card is simply there. Nothing flies, grows or fades.
  2. Should work Closing is instant too, and ← → switch walks without sliding.
  3. Your judgement (Optional) With the real device setting turned on, frames 1–3 also open and close instantly, without reloading this page.

5. Rapid repeated clicks and interruptions

The buttons in the frame run quick sequences by themselves: double-click, ten fast clicks, Esc or Back half way, clicking a card as it flies home, random input. Try the same by hand too. Browser history is on here, so Back closes the card.

What to look for

  1. Should work After each sequence settles, the dark status bar at the bottom says “clean ✓” (press Esc first if a card is left open).
  2. Should work Clicking a card while it shrinks home turns it around smoothly instead of restarting.
  3. Your judgement Nothing ever gets stuck, flickers, or ends up in the wrong place.

6. Detail photos that are still downloading

On a real site the thumbnail and the big detail photo are different files, and the big one only starts downloading when you click. Here the big photos take 1.5 seconds to arrive the first time each card opens (they're remembered after that).

What to look for

  1. Problem I found First open of each card: the thumbnail vanishes and an empty grey box (with the photo's description text in its corner) zooms up instead of the picture. The photo pops in later.
  2. Should work Second open of the same card: the picture flies properly.

7. Content inside a card that scrolls sideways

The detail cards contain a photo strip and a wide table, each meant to scroll sideways on its own. Open a card, scroll down a little to them, and try to scroll them sideways (two-finger sideways swipe on a trackpad, or Shift + mouse wheel).

What to look for

  1. Problem I found The strip and the table don't scroll sideways; the swipe switches to the next walk instead.

8. A large gallery (48 walks)

Opening any card also builds the cards for every walk in the group, so the first moment of the zoom does more work the bigger the gallery. Watch the “slowest frame” number in the status bar (17 ms is perfectly smooth; over about 50 ms is a visible hitch). On a fast computer it may look fine; on a mid-range phone it hitches.

What to look for

  1. Your judgement Note the “slowest frame” number after opening a card here, and compare it with scenario 1 (9 walks).
  2. Your judgement The picture hesitates for a moment before it starts to move (compare with scenario 1).

9. The same walk shown twice (featured + grid)

The walk at the top (“Tea Terraces”, shown big as a featured item) also appears in the grid below. Click the big featured picture.

What to look for

  1. Problem I found The zoom does not start from the picture you clicked: the featured picture stays where it is, and the flying picture comes from the grid copy lower down (from below the bottom of the window).

How to turn on reduced motion on your device

Turn it on, come back to this page, and open a card in scenarios 1–3. They should open and close instantly. No reload is needed.

Mac

  • System Settings → Accessibility → Display → turn on Reduce motion.

iPhone / iPad

  • Settings → Accessibility → Motion → turn on Reduce Motion.

Windows 11

  • Settings → Accessibility → Visual effects → turn off Animation effects.

Windows 10

  • Settings → Ease of Access → Display → turn off Show animations in Windows.

Android

  • Settings → Accessibility → turn on Remove animations (on some phones: Accessibility → Visibility enhancements, or Color and motion).

Without changing your settings (Chrome or Edge)

  • Open DevTools (F12 or Cmd+Option+I), press Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+P, type reduced motion, and choose “Emulate CSS prefers-reduced-motion: reduce”. It applies to this tab only while DevTools is open.

The status bar inside each frame shows “reduced motion: on (your device)” when the setting is detected.
